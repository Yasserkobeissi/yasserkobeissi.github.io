02 / Who · Qui est-ce

Loud in the roomprécis dans le fichier

Born in Beirut, working in Riyadh. The one in the office everyone hears first and asks last, because by then it is already drawn. "I'm a fun guy," he says, and the room agrees. Leadership, management and strategy are what he calls his strongest skills, and he stays deep in the design itself: how a thing starts, how it finishes, how an idea becomes something you can stand inside. On weekends he drifts cars. Not a metaphor.

07 / From marker to room
STEP 01 / 04

Sketch

Everything starts on paper with two markers, a grey and the client's colour. If the idea does not hold up in one drawing, it will not hold up in a hall.

STEP 02 / 04

Plan

Dimensions, sightlines, the path a visitor walks. A booth is a small building with a very short life, and it gets planned like one.

STEP 03 / 04

Render

Cinema 4D and Lumion. Light placed the way it will be rigged, materials as they will be printed. The render is a promise, so it has to be buildable.

STEP 04 / 04

Built

The hall, the crowd, the lights on. The only version that counts.
