# v10 audit: the ten weakest moments

Checked in the browser at 1440×900 and 390×844 on 23 October 2026. No code changed. Ranked, weakest first.

1. **No document skeleton.** `index.html` starts at `<title>`: no doctype, no `<html>`/`<head>`, no charset, no viewport meta. The page runs in quirks mode, every `·`, `→`, curly quote and the Arabic lines render as mojibake when served without a charset header, and a phone gets the 980 px desktop layout (none of the 720/860 px breakpoints ever fire). Fix: add the skeleton with `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover`.
2. **Contact is a sidebar.** The closing chapter is a 380 px column pinned to the left of a 1440 px canvas with the right 60 % empty; paragraph, CTA, switch and footer all stack in it. Fix: two-column grid, the serif paragraph at a 40–45ch measure and larger size, CTA and contact row across the full width.
3. **Timeline is too long and collides with itself.** 6,500 px of pin for 11 entries; the sticky "Twenty years" title sits on the year numerals, the polaroid covers the last column's copy, and the 2006 panel's text is dark grey on black. Fix: pin to about 3.5 screens, title in a header band, polaroid in the empty top-right, 2006 panel on paper.
4. **A paper wedge sits over the hero.** `.work` has no `position`, so its wipe anchors to the document and draws a paper triangle at the top of the page on load; Reel→Work gets no wipe. Fix: `position:relative` on `.work`.
5. **Body text has no middle size.** Who, Cars, Live, Process and Contact set ~15 px copy in 300–400 px columns next to 150 px display type. Fix: a type scale with a 19–20 px body, 55–65ch measures, and one lede size between body and display.
6. **Helix caption fights the cards.** The project title sits on top of the front card, and on phones the title, "Open on Behance" button and scroll hint overlap. Fix: reserve a bottom band for title (left) and button (right) and keep cards above it.
7. **Build opens as shards and ends as a toy.** Assembly starts with giant flat planes slicing across the screen, ends on cyan/red boxes on grey, and the parts list overprints the dot rail. Fix: parts enter small and off-screen, shard planes dimmed, materials in paper/ink/tally, parts list moved in from the rail.
8. **Reel is a sliver.** A 500 px band wedged between Lights and Work with the Lights photo bleeding over its top; it reads as leftover, not a chapter. Fix: a full viewport, dark, strip centred, one caption line, with Lights handing off before it.
9. **Hero corners pile up.** Lede, the rail's START label and REC share the top-right; on 390 px the eyebrow overprints the nav and timecode. Fix: one element per corner, eyebrow below the title on phones.
10. **Services plate covers the line you hover.** The image plate lands on the word under the cursor, and the YK mark overprints the slate label top-left. Fix: offset the plate to the right of the cursor and clamp to the right half; slate labels below the nav line.

Also noticed, not in the ten: Drift tells phone users "mouse = steering" and cannot be steered by touch; in Snaps the pile overlaps the Flash button and chapter label on phones; the Voice pull-quote marks render at headline size; Object and Work feature titles overprint their captions at 390 px; the Work index hover preview covers neighbouring titles.
