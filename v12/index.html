Drawn first,
built as drawnfirst in the room, last to ask

Born in Beirut, working in Riyadh. Warm, quick and sure: I say what I think and I draw it before the meeting ends. Leadership, management and strategy are what I call my strongest skills, and I stay deep in the design itself: how a thing starts, how it finishes, how an idea becomes something you can stand inside. On weekends I drift cars. Not a metaphor.
