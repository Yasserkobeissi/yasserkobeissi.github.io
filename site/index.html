About

I'm Yasser. I grew up in Beirut and I work in Riyadh, where I lead the creative team at Sovyal Events.

I design events, exhibition booths and the gift items that go with them, and I produce the screen content that plays on them. I started on television channels in Beirut, spent ten years on motion design in Beirut production houses, then ran the creative department at SBA Group in Riyadh, across four television channels.

I'm a passionate designer. I studied graphic design at the Lebanese American University. I'm crazy about cars, I drift, and I like a big operation with a big team.

How I lead

Teams, plans
and the long game

Creative work on this scale is run, not just drawn. I have led creative departments for a decade, and I keep training for it.

Leadership

Leadership, for me, is making the room braver than it was when I walked in. I lead the creative team at Sovyal Events today; before that I ran the creative department at SBA Group across four television channels, and I was art director and chief executive at Thakwa Events.

A team that trusts the plan will take the risk with you.
Planning

A booth or an event is a schedule before it is a picture. I plan the build, the crews and the days, and I hold the plan when the brief moves. I trained as a planning specialist because a good idea on a bad timeline is still a bad idea.

The render is the easy part. The calendar is the design.
Management

Big operations, big teams, many suppliers. I keep the work moving and the client calm, from the first sketch to the last screw. The managerial leadership diplomas above are the theory; twenty years of opening nights are the practice.

Calm is a deliverable.
