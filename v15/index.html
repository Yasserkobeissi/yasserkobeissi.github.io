About

I'm Yasser. I grew up in Beirut and I work in Riyadh, where I lead the creative at Sovyal Events.

I design events, exhibition stands and the objects inside them. Before that I spent ten years on motion design in Beirut production houses and ran the creative for four television channels in Riyadh.

I studied graphic design at the Lebanese American University. I'm crazy about cars, I drift, and I like a big operation with a big team.
